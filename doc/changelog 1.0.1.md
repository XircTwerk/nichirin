CHANGELOG FOR BREATH OF NICHIRIN V1.0.1

Thank you guys for waiting so long, and i promise this update won't disappoint

Heads up! azurelib is now a new dependency.

**_NEW FEATURES:_**
* Added gui screen, that displays player information, that also serves as a hub for managing your player.
* Added an attack wheel, that lets you cast your attacks from your breathing style
* Movement system, added dashing, dodging, air dodging, and backstepping
* Blocking/parrying, cool system that i personally love. includes a new "stance bar" and if your stance bar gets broken then you get stunned.

**_BREATHING STYLES:_**
* Thunder breathing! the first breathing style. it has 7 moves. 6 of them on the attack wheel and 1 of them using a right click input. also has a crouching variant!
* To obtain thunder breathing, use the command /breathing add {playername} thunder_breathing, or get struck by lightning with no armor on!
* When you get the achievement, "baptized by the storm", you know that youve gotten thunder breathing.

**_ITEMS:_**
* Thunder katana. katanas in this mod all function the same, making variants cosmetic. breathing styles are changed in the gui screen.
